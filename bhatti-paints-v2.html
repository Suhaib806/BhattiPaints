Our Heritage · Bahawalpur, Pakistan 1972 — Present
1972

Over Five Decades of Colour.

Established in 1972 in the historic city of Bahawalpur, Bhatti Paints (Pvt.) Ltd. began with a clear purpose: to bring dependable paint solutions and expressive colour to the homes and commercial spaces of our community.

Across generations, painting a home in Pakistan has always marked moments of renewal—welcoming family, celebrating milestones, and caring for the spaces where life unfolds. Our heritage is built on those personal relationships with homeowners, master painters, contractors, and architects.
